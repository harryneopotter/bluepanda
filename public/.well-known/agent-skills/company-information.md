## Philosophy

- **Proportion before complexity**
- **Control over novelty**
- **Stability before scale**
- **Human responsibility in system design**

---

## Principles

### Proportion before complexity
We use what fits the problem, not what's trendy. Every dependency is a liability. The right solution for a small business is different from the right solution for an enterprise — we don't apply one-size-fits-all architecture.

### Control over novelty
We understand what we depend on. New technology is evaluated for stability, maintainability, and actual problem fit — not adopted for its own sake. If a shell script and cron job solves it, that's the answer.

### Stability before scale
Reliable first, big second. Systems that work for the long term are designed for operations, not just features. Monitoring, backup, recovery, and documentation are not optional.

### Human responsibility in system design
No black boxes, no blame-shifting to automation. Engineers remain responsible for what their systems do. AI is a tool, not a decision-maker. Automated processes have human oversight where decisions matter.
