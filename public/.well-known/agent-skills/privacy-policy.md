# Blue Panda — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 July 2026

## Who operates this site

Blue Panda Hosting and Designs is a sole proprietorship operated by Sachin. Privacy questions can be sent to `contact@bluepanda.in`.

## Information received

The contact form receives a name, email address, and message. Submissions are sent to the business mailbox and retained there as business correspondence for communication, support, and record-keeping.

AI Architect sends a system description and selected analysis depth to a server-side function and then to Google Gemini to generate a blueprint. The website does not provide a user account or application database for storing prompts.

Hosting, email, AI, and serverless providers may process technical request data or retain operational logs under their own policies.

## Use of information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide requested support, generate AI Architect blueprints, operate and secure the website, and meet applicable business or legal obligations. Contact submissions are not used to create a marketing newsletter or sell personal information.

## Service providers

The current deployment uses Netlify for hosting and serverless functions, Brevo SMTP for contact-message delivery, and Google Gemini for AI Architect generation. The hosting environment may later move to a virtual machine; this policy will be updated if processing changes.

## AI Architect warning

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, API keys, private keys, personal data, confidential client information, or other sensitive material to AI Architect. Generated blueprints are informational and require human review. The tool does not make infrastructure changes or guarantee production readiness.

## Retention, security, and rights

Contact emails are retained in the business mailbox as reasonably needed for communication, support, business records, or legal obligations. AI Architect prompts are not intentionally stored in a Blue Panda application database; provider logs, mailbox backups, and third-party retention follow relevant provider settings and policies.

For questions, access, correction, or deletion requests where applicable, email `contact@bluepanda.in`. We may verify identity before handling a request. Rights depend on applicable law.

Blue Panda may update this policy when the site, providers, or data practices change.
